LPG

LPG Generator Set

20–1,250 kVA. LPG is widely distributed in Nigeria, easy to store and portable. Switching cuts power-generation fuel costs by about 58%.

  • More environmentally friendly: low emissions for a greener impact.
  • More cost-effective: lean combustion yields up to 5 kWh from 1 kg of LPG.
  • Safer: precise ignition, flame arresters and explosion-proof valves.
  • More convenient: abundant refuelling, cylinder swap, one-button start.
  • Containerized customisation: units can be synchronised for varying loads.
CNG

CNG Generator Set

20–4,000 kVA, with CNG cylinder groups and PRMS. Nigeria’s expanding gas pipeline network makes CNG a long-term saving of up to 72%.

  • More reliable: mature technology and processes.
  • More efficient: peak generator efficiency of up to 44%.
  • Environmentally friendly: lower emissions than diesel.
  • More durable: built for 24-hour duty, with a 15-year lifespan and 30,000-hour major service intervals.

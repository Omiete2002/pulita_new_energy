Why Pulita?

Why switch to gas with Pulita.

LPG and CNG generators from Pulita are cleaner, cheaper to run, safer and simpler to operate than the diesel sets they replace.

01

More Environmentally Friendly

Gas burns cleaner than diesel: lower CO₂, NOₓ and particulate emissions for a greener footprint — and fewer complaints from neighbours.

02

More Cost-Effective

Lean-combustion technology produces up to 5 kWh of electricity from 1 kg of LPG, cutting your cost per kWh by 50–65% versus diesel.

03

Safer

Precise electronic ignition, flame arresters and explosion-proof valves are built into every set, backed by ISO 45001 safety management.

04

More Convenient

Refuel from abundant LPG/CNG supply or swap cylinders. One-button start, remote monitoring and no diesel theft to worry about.
